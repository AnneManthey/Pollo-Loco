Story

It was a peaceful, sunny afternoon...

But suddenly, disaster struck!

The ruthless Chicken King and his endless army of crazy chickens raided the village. They scattered Pepe's precious gold coins everywhere and stole the legendary recipe for the hottest Salsa in the world! Now, Pepe is wide awake – and furious. Armed only with his jumping skills and empty Salsa bottles to throw at his feathered enemies, he must fight his way through the desert.
